## 1. Quick Start Walkthrough

Getting started with HyperClick Pro 2026 takes less than 30 seconds:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [ STEP 1: Launch ] ──▶ [ STEP 2: Pick Mode ] ──▶ [ STEP 3: Press F6 ]   |
| Run HyperClick Pro      Select CPS / Preset        Toggle ON/OFF in-game|
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **Launch HyperClick Pro**: Open the application via Start Menu, Desktop shortcut, or portable binary.
2. **Select Target CPS or Gaming Preset**:
   - Choose a preset from the top dropdown (e.g., *Minecraft Butterfly* or *Roblox Blade Ball*).
   - Alternatively, adjust the **Interval Slider** to your desired milliseconds ($ms$) or clicks-per-second ($CPS$).
3. **Select Click Button & Mode**: Choose **Left Click**, **Right Click**, **Middle Click**, or **Double Click**.
4. **Enter Your Game / Application**: Switch to your game window.
5. **Press <kbd>F6</kbd> to Start/Stop**: HyperClick Pro will begin clicking instantly. The mechanical audio synthesizer will provide acoustic feedback, and the Mini HUD will track your live CPS.
6. **Press <kbd>F9</kbd> in Emergency**: If you need to immediately stop all automation, press the <kbd>F9</kbd> Emergency Killswitch.

## 3. Single-Point Auto-Clicker Engine

The Single-Point Click Engine is the core clicking pipeline:

### 3.1 Interval Configuration
HyperClick Pro allows precision down to the microsecond level ($\mu\text{s}$):
- **Hours, Minutes, Seconds**: For slow automation (e.g., clicking once every 10 minutes to prevent AFK timeouts).
- **Milliseconds ($ms$)**: The standard unit for gamer CPS configuration.
  $$\text{CPS} = \frac{1000}{\text{Interval in ms}}$$
  - $50\text{ ms} = 20\text{ CPS}$
  - $20\text{ ms} = 50\text{ CPS}$
  - $10\text{ ms} = 100\text{ CPS}$
  - $2\text{ ms} = 500\text{ CPS}$
- **Microseconds ($\mu s$)**: For sub-millisecond hyper-speed bursts up to 500+ CPS.

### 3.2 Click Modes & Buttons
- **Left Click (`MOUSEEVENTF_LEFTDOWN` / `UP`)**: Primary attack/action click.
- **Right Click (`MOUSEEVENTF_RIGHTDOWN` / `UP`)**: Secondary interaction/bridging/aiming.
- **Middle Click (`MOUSEEVENTF_MIDDLEDOWN` / `UP`)**: Scroll-wheel click.
- **Double Click**: Sends two rapid left clicks within 15ms of each other.
- **Triple Click**: Sends three clicks in rapid succession.
- **Hold Down Mode**: Keeps the mouse button depressed continuously until toggled off.

### 3.3 Repeat Limits vs Continuous Loop
- **Infinite (Until Stopped)**: Clicks continuously until <kbd>F6</kbd> or <kbd>F9</kbd> is triggered.
- **Fixed Count**: Stops automatically after sending an exact number of clicks (e.g., exactly 100 clicks).
- **Time Duration Limit**: Runs for a specified timeframe (e.g., run for 45 seconds, then stop).

## 5. Gaussian Humanizer 2.0 & Anti-Cheat Evasion

Modern anti-cheat systems (such as *Hypixel Watchdog*, *Roblox Hyperion/Byfron*, *Easy Anti-Cheat*, and *Vanguard*) do not just check if clicking is fast—they analyze the **statistical entropy** and **standard deviation** of click timing intervals.

```

         ▲                                       ▲
         │       ___                             │       │
         │     /     \                           │       │
         │    /       \                          │       │
Frequency│  /           \               Frequency│       │
         │ /             \                       │       │
         └─────────────────►                     └───────┴─────────►
           Interval (ms)                           Interval (ms)
           [μ = 50ms, σ = 4ms]                     [Fixed 50.00ms BAN!]
```

### 5.1 The Mathematical Model (Box-Muller Transform)
When Gaussian Humanizer is enabled, click delays are sampled dynamically from a normal distribution curve $N(\mu, \sigma^2)$:

$$T_{\text{delay}} = \mu + \sigma \cdot \sqrt{-2 \ln(U_1)} \cos(2\pi U_2)$$

Where:
- $\mu$ is your configured base click interval (e.g. 55ms for ~18 CPS).
- $\sigma$ is the Standard Deviation parameter (configurable 0.5ms to 8.0ms).
- $U_1, U_2$ are uniform random variables $\in (0, 1)$.

### 5.2 Spatial Jitter Scatter
Physical human clicks never land on the exact same pixel 1,000 times consecutively. HyperClick Pro introduces configurable **Spatial Jitter**:
- Injects small, sub-pixel or multi-pixel offsets ($\pm 1\text{ to }5\text{ px}$) around the target center.
- Distributes clicks in a radial Gaussian bell curve around the target coordinate.

### 5.3 Micro-Fatigue Cycles
Human muscles experience fatigue and rhythm breaks. HyperClick Pro can inject:
- **Periodic Hesitations**: Every 20–80 clicks, a minor 15–40ms pause is simulated.
- **Fatigue Degradation**: Over a 5-minute continuous session, average CPS naturally drifts down by 3–5%, exactly mimicking human finger exhaustion.

## 9. Emergency Killswitch & Failsafes

Safety is paramount when running high-speed automation:

- **<kbd>F9</kbd> Hard Killswitch**: Instantly shuts down all native click threads, timers, and active macros.
- **Screen Boundary Failsafe**: If your cursor hits any screen corner $(0, 0)$, HyperClick Pro can be configured to automatically disengage.
- **Global Shortcut Priority**: The Electron Main process captures hotkeys via low-level OS hooks, ensuring hotkeys trigger even if a game is freezing.
